UnityESS Aura: A Commercial and Industrial Battery Storage System for Business Continuity

November 24, 20266 min read

A commercial and industrial battery energy storage system (BESS) helps businesses store electricity and use it when the grid supply is interrupted or when energy costs are higher. UnityESS Aura is a cabinet-based commercial and industrial battery storage system designed for businesses in India. The published product range is 100–260 kWh, with 50–100 kW power options. It uses LFP battery technology, supports switchover in under 20 milliseconds and is designed for applications such as factories, warehouses, hospitals, commercial buildings and EV charging infrastructure.

For businesses assessing a diesel generator alternative, UnityESS Aura can support backup power, peak-load management and better use of solar energy. The right configuration depends on the facility's load profile, required backup duration and operating conditions.

How Power Cuts Affect Businesses

A power interruption can affect production schedules, refrigeration, IT infrastructure and essential building services. In a factory, an unexpected outage may interrupt a production process. In a hospital, a dependable battery backup is essential for critical equipment. In a cold storage facility, maintaining temperature is essential to protect stored goods.

Diesel generator sets are widely used for backup, but they involve fuel, regular maintenance, emissions and a start-up interval. A battery energy storage system offers another way to manage short interruptions and energy demand. Depending on system sizing and site requirements, it can reduce reliance on diesel generation and help businesses manage their electricity use more strategically.

What Is UnityESS Aura?

UnityESS Aura is a commercial and industrial battery energy storage system designed for businesses that need backup power, peak-load support or renewable-energy integration. Ornate Solar and UnityESS list Aura in 100–260 kWh configurations, with power options ranging from 50 kW to 100 kW.

The system uses lithium iron phosphate (LFP) battery cells and an air-cooled, cabinet-based design. Its IP54-rated enclosure is intended for indoor and outdoor installation, subject to site and installation requirements. Multiple units can be considered where a facility's energy requirement exceeds the capacity of a single cabinet.

How UnityESS Aura Manages Energy

A BESS is more than a battery cabinet. A complete installation brings together battery cells, a battery management system (BMS), power conversion equipment, thermal management, protection systems and energy management controls. These components work together to charge, store and discharge energy in line with the site’s operating requirements.

UnityESS uses its energy management platform, Unity EMS, to monitor system performance and manage charging and discharging strategies. Depending on the project design, the system can be configured to support peak-load management, backup power and the integration of on-site solar generation.

How UnityESS Aura Helps Businesses

1. Backup power during grid interruptions

UnityESS publishes a switchover time of under 20 milliseconds for Aura. This fast transfer can help support sensitive electrical loads when the grid supply fails. The actual outcome depends on system configuration, connected equipment, protection settings and the load being supported.

2. Peak-load management

A battery storage system can charge when electricity is available and discharge during selected high-demand periods. This can help a business manage peak demand, subject to its tariff structure, load profile, battery capacity and dispatch strategy.

3. Reduced dependence on diesel generation

By supplying energy from stored electricity during suitable operating periods, UnityESS Aura can reduce generator runtime. Whether it can replace a diesel generator for a particular facility depends on the required power, backup duration, critical-load profile and site-specific design. It should not be assumed to replace every generator application without an engineering assessment.

4. Better use of on-site solar power

When designed to work with a solar installation, battery storage can retain surplus solar energy for use later, including periods when solar generation is low. This can increase the usefulness of on-site renewable electricity and help align energy supply with a facility’s demand. Where Can Businesses Use UnityESS Aura?

UnityESS Aura is positioned for commercial and industrial facilities with defined backup, energy-management or solar-integration requirements. Potential applications include:

  • Factories and manufacturing units that need a BESS for factories to support production loads.
  • Warehouses and commercial buildings seeking backup and peak-demand management.
  • Hospitals and healthcare facilities that need battery backup for hospitals with critical electrical loads.
  • Data centres and server facilities that require carefully engineered power continuity for data centres.
  • Hotels and large commercial facilities with variable demand.
  • EV charging stations that need support for charging loads and peak management.
  • Cold storage facilities where refrigeration continuity is important, subject to load and backup-duration assessment.

How to Choose the Right UnityESS System

Not every facility has the same power requirement. A site assessment should consider the connected load in kW, the energy required in kWh, the duration of backup, the nature of critical loads, the electricity tariff, available solar generation and the space available for installation.

UnityESS Aura is intended for commercial-scale applications. For larger industrial storage requirements, businesses can also review UnityESS Aqua, while UnityESS Ultima is positioned for utility-scale projects. The most suitable system should be selected through an engineering assessment rather than capacity alone.
